When to use yellow

Reserved for "look here right now" — the one number on the page you most need a user to act on, or to mark the brand colour itself on the swatch grid. Never as a fill for buttons, charts, or large surfaces; never alongside another saturated colour. If everything is highlighted, nothing is.
